\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

\paragraph{Scenario.}
On a production line every item is photographed, and quality-control staff must decide whether it ships.
Our detector serves these inspectors: it scores each image, and an alert ($s(x) \ge \tau$) rejects the
item or sends it to manual re-inspection.
A missed defect reaches a customer; a false alarm costs inspection labour, so the threshold must trade
the two at the real defect rate of the line, around 1\%.

\paragraph{Motivation and anomaly definition.}
In this image anomaly detection task, an anomaly is an item with a surface or structural defect, such
as a bent pin on an otherwise normal circuit board (pcb1 in Figure~\ref{fig:samples}).
The task is hard because defects are rare and varied, so models must learn from normal images; they are
tiny (median 0.20\% of the image); and on some objects several instances lie at random positions, so
the normal appearance itself varies.
We study this on VisA \citep{zou2022spot}, 12 objects photographed under inspection conditions.

\paragraph{Research question.}
How well does a location-wise Gaussian on pretrained patch features (PaDiM, our Tier~2) detect defects
when it is fitted on normal images only, how far is it from a strong reference (PatchCore, Tier~3), and
what would its alerts cost an inspection team at a 1\% defect rate?

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item A leakage-free three-tier comparison with per-object thresholds: Tier~3 beats Tier~2 by
    $+0.084$ PR-AUC (significant), yet at a 1\% defect rate only 2.4\% and 4.6\% of their alerts are
    real defects (\S\ref{sec:results}).
  \item Ablations showing that the location-wise covariance carries Tier~2 (up to 0.12 PR-AUC), while
    2\% contamination and the aggregation rule do not matter (\S\ref{sec:results}).
  \item An error analysis tracing Tier~2's failures to position: its map hits the defect for 25\% of
    anomalies, Tier~3's for 70\% (\S\ref{sec:analysis}).
  \item A data audit that flags an acquisition shortcut in three objects before testing
    (\S\ref{sec:eda}).
\end{itemize}
