\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{Split and leakage prevention.}
All tiers use the split of \S\ref{sec:data} (fixed indices, seed~0; 7,705 / 1,194 / 1,682 images for
train / val / test).
Models are fitted on train normals only, normalisation uses fixed ImageNet statistics, and val
anomalies only choose $\tau$ and hyperparameters.
For every object, all models and thresholds are fixed before test features are loaded, and test is
evaluated once.
The only earlier contact with test was a pipeline smoke test on one object (candle, seed~0, default
settings) before the search; nothing was chosen from it.

\paragraph{Hyperparameters.}
On val (seed~0) we fit the full grid per tier and keep the setting with the highest mean val PR-AUC
over the 12 objects.
Tier~2: $d \in \{50, 100, 200\}$ and layers $\in \{\{2,3\}, \{2,3,4\}\}$, selected $d = 200$ with
layers $\{2,3,4\}$ (val PR-AUC 0.664; at the grid edge, which we kept as defined in advance);
$\epsilon = 0.01$ fixed.
Tier~3: coreset $\in \{1\%, 10\%\}$ and the same layer sets, selected 1\% with $\{2,3\}$ (0.811);
$3 \times 3$ neighbourhood and 128-d projection fixed.

\paragraph{Metrics.}
PR-AUC is primary because it reflects how many alerts are real defects under imbalance
\citep{davis2006relationship,saito2015precision}; ROC-AUC allows comparison with the literature.
At $\tau$ we report F1, F2, the false-positive rate (good products sent to re-inspection) and
$\mathrm{prec}_\pi$ (Eq.~\ref{eq:prec-pi}) at $\pi = 1\%$ and $5\%$ (share of alerts that are real
defects in production).
Numbers are the mean $\pm$ std over seeds 0--2 of the 12-object average; the seed sets the Tier~2
channel subset and the Tier~3 coreset start and projection (Tier~1 is deterministic).

\paragraph{Implementation and hardware.}
PyTorch 2.15.0 (CUDA 13.2), torchvision 0.29.2 and scikit-learn on an NVIDIA RTX 4060 Ti (8\,GB).
Latency is the median over 50 val images of pcb1 on GPU, batch size~1, after 10 warm-up images,
including feature extraction; training time covers model fitting only.
Peak memory was 2.24\,GB GPU and 6.6\,GB RAM.
